# Email Management

Use this skill when you need to draft, organize, and respond to professional communication across a business or team.

## Core abilities
- Write clear subject lines and concise email summaries
- Structure messages for action, urgency, and follow-up
- Draft outreach, status updates, and client communication
- Keep tone professional, friendly, and results-oriented

## Suggested workflow
1. Define the goal of the email.
2. Identify the audience and desired action.
3. Draft a short opening, key context, and clear request.
4. Close with next steps and deadlines.
5. Review for tone, clarity, and completeness.

## Example prompt
"Draft a professional follow-up email to a client who requested a proposal update. Keep it concise, polite, and action-oriented."
