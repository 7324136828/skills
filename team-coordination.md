# Team Coordination

Use this skill when aligning multiple stakeholders, tracking decisions, and keeping work moving.

## Core abilities
- Schedule and facilitate meetings effectively
- Share concise status updates and action items
- Align stakeholders around timelines and priorities
- Resolve communication gaps and follow-ups

## Suggested workflow
1. Clarify the purpose and required participants.
2. Share goals, agenda, and deadlines.
3. Capture decisions and follow-up actions.
4. Close loops with concise reporting and reminders.

## Example prompt
"Summarize project updates for a cross-functional team and highlight risks, decisions, and next actions."
