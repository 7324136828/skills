# Genomic Health

Use this skill when interpreting genetic data, ancestry DNA results, or lab-reported variants.

## Core abilities
- Explain genetic variants and their associated health implications in plain language
- Summarize ancestry DNA health reports (23andMe, AncestryDNA, etc.)
- Review pharmacogenomic markers to flag potential drug–gene interactions
- Produce provider-ready summaries of raw VCF or panel results

## Suggested workflow
1. Identify the data format (raw DNA file, variant report, VCF, PDF panel result).
2. Clarify the user's question: ancestry insights, disease risk, or medication response.
3. Look up relevant variants against curated databases (ClinVar, PharmGKB, dbSNP).
4. Produce a plain-language explanation and recommend follow-up with a genetic counselor.

## Example prompt
"I received a raw DNA download from 23andMe. Can you explain which variants are flagged for health relevance, what they mean, and what questions I should ask my doctor?"
