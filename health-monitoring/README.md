# Health Monitoring

Use this skill when tracking, reviewing, or acting on personal health data over time.

## Core abilities
- Log and trend vital signs (blood pressure, heart rate, weight, sleep)
- Journal symptoms and share structured summaries with care providers
- Manage medication schedules, doses, and adherence streaks
- Spot patterns in wearable or manual data exports

## Suggested workflow
1. Identify the data source (wearable export, manual log, app CSV, PDF lab result).
2. Load or paste the data and ask for a summary or trend analysis.
3. Flag anything outside the user's personal baselines or reference ranges.
4. Generate a provider-ready summary or a plain-language explanation.

## Example prompt
"Here is a CSV export of my last 90 days of blood pressure readings. Summarize the trend, flag any readings outside the normal range, and draft a one-page summary I can bring to my next appointment."
